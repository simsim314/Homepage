CONSTRUCTION PIECES FOR MAKING CA TILES — MICHAEL SIMKIN

Ten separate STL parts reconstructed from my March 21, 2019 OpenJSCAD design.
Original discussion: https://conwaylife.com/forums/viewtopic.php?p=73650#p73650

WHAT THIS SET IS
The kit supplies pieces for making configurable tiles. It is a lower-level kit,
separate from the 26-type Conway's Life construction described earlier in the
thread. These ten files do not constitute a complete assembled Life simulation.

FILES
side-plain.stl           Plain side panel
side-connecting.stl      Side panel with outward connection features
top-plain.stl            Plain top
top-attachment.stl       Top with attachment features
top-zero.stl             Top with a zero-valued output connector
top-one.stl              Top with a one-valued output connector
bottom-attachment.stl    Base/core with attachment input
bottom-zero.stl          Base/core with zero-valued input
bottom-one.stl           Base/core with one-valued input
bottom-either.stl        Base/core accepting either value

Each bottom part includes the core. Side panels and a top attach around it.
The article's viewer shows bottom-zero and top-one with adjustable separation.
Their nominal mating height is 39 mm at the export scale.

SCALE AND PRINTING
STL coordinates are interpreted in millimetres. The exports use a uniform 3x
scale relative to the historical code: its 10-unit core becomes 30 mm. This
preserves the design's relative geometry, including its original clearances.
All parts have been translated onto Z=0 for import into a slicer.

Treat this as an experimental kit. No new physical print or fit test has been
performed. Mesh validation does not establish printer tolerances, strength, or
successful assembly. Inspect connector overhangs in your slicer and choose an
orientation appropriate to your printer.

Start with one base, one top and one plain side. Check those connections before
printing multiples. The original clearances are small; printer-specific fit may
require a revised tolerance parameter rather than simply scaling one part alone.
Do not rescale mating parts independently.

VALIDATION AND EXPORT CHANGES
All ten exports are closed meshes with consistent winding, positive volume and
one solid component. The connecting-side export contained a detached flat
two-triangle fragment from the legacy boolean calculation; only that zero-
thickness fragment was removed. The solid part was retained.
A nominal top/base intersection check found approximately 0.01 cubic mm of
overlap in the tessellated historical geometry. Physical fit remains untested.

REPRODUCIBILITY

manifest.json records dimensions, source coordinates and export scale.
mesh-validation.json records digital checks, not physical testing.
Website source includes scripts/export-tile-kit.mjs to reproduce the exports.
