Title: Construction pieces for making CA tiles — original 2019 OpenJSCAD design

These ten parts are basic construction pieces for making tiles. A core, sides
and input/output faces make configurable cubes; cubes are combined into actual
tiles that encode local computational rules. I developed the design in the 2019 ConwayLife
discussion "Realizing CGOL a 3d tiling", alongside work by Paul Callahan and
Adam Goucher on physical tiling and cellular automata.

The base contains the central core. Side panels and a top attach around it.
Different inputs and outputs provide zero, one, either-value and attachment
connections. The supplied CAD source allows the pieces to be inspected and
modified.

This is the modular kit, not a ready-made set of the 26 Life tiles. The Life
construction, its diagrams and reductions are described separately in the thread.

The STL exports are at 3x the original code's scale, with a 30 mm core, and use
millimetres. They have closed, consistently wound meshes. One detached flat
fragment in the legacy export was removed without changing the solid part.

Experimental design: print and fit have not been newly tested. Start with a
base, a top and a plain side before printing more. The original clearances may
need adjustment for your printer. See PRINT-NOTES.txt for scale, limitations and
the full file list. Source and digital mesh-check results are included.
