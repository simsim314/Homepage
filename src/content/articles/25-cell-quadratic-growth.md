I found this **25-cell quadratic-growth pattern on October 21, 2014**. Its initial population improved on the previous 26-cell record, which had stood for eight years.

## A small seed, continuing growth

The size here counts live cells in the initial configuration. They are spread across a large bounding box: a small population does not necessarily mean a compact pattern.

LifeWiki classifies the result as a breeder. Its long-term population grows quadratically as the evolving pattern generates further growing components.

## The next discoveries

The 25-cell record lasted two days. I found a [24-cell pattern](/articles/24-cell-quadratic-growth/) on October 23, then [switch engine ping-pong](/articles/switch-engine-ping-pong/) with 23 cells on October 29.

These are separate patterns with their own downloadable RLE files. They describe the history of the search, rather than current minimum-population records. LifeWiki links the original report and supplies this pattern's file.
