Chess960 randomizes the back rank subject to placement constraints. Chess960x960 explores positions where the two sides use independent starting arrangements, creating asymmetric games from the outset.

## Why evaluate the starting position?

An unfamiliar arrangement can be interesting without being balanced. The tool uses Stockfish to estimate the advantage in a starting position, so you can explore both the layout and its evaluation.

The combinatorial space comes from pairing the two sides' Chess960 setups. A score near equality is an engine assessment at the selected depth, not a proof that the position is balanced.

## The implemented tool

The interface generates a new position, displays it on a board, and evaluates it. You can change the search depth and copy its FEN for use in another chess tool.

Evaluation-range buttons search for positions in bands from a decisive Black advantage to a decisive White advantage. A Stop Search control halts the process.

## Try it

Load the original browser app below. Check its Stockfish readiness indicator before searching. Higher depth takes more time; the README explains the controls and the sign convention for scores.

This is an exploration tool for starting positions. The article does not claim a tournament ruleset, a fairness guarantee, or new engine results.
