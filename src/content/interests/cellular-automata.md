## Exploring other rules

Cellular automata connect local rules with large-scale behavior. This collection focuses on rules beyond Conway’s Game of Life and experiments with learned local updates.

## Construction and growth

In Serizawa, I built a [Geminoid](/articles/serizawa-geminoid/) and found a [four-cell quadratic-growth seed](/articles/serizawa-quadratic-growth/). [Geminoid Particles](/articles/geminoid-particles/) uses a rule I designed to explore construction and self-destruction. The [c/3 spaceship in Live Free or Die](/articles/live-free-or-die-c3-spaceship/) is another example of what a different rule makes possible.

## Learned local updates

[Neural CA MNIST](/articles/neural-ca-mnist/) connects this interest with AI: learned, step-specific local updates evolve a digit image through a sequence of states.

Conway’s Life discoveries, search tools, and physical Life constructions have their own [Conway’s Game of Life collection](/interests/game-of-life/).
