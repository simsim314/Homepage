Changing a cellular automaton's rules changes its building blocks. A reaction that is difficult in Conway’s Life may be easy elsewhere; other constraints appear in its place.

My work in other rules includes a universal-constructor-based pattern in Serizawa, small Serizawa seeds with quadratic growth, and a prize-winning spaceship in Live Free or Die. These are different rules, with their own signals, catalysts, and construction techniques.

The connections are useful too. The Serizawa construction discussion explored how ideas about colliding signals and construction arms could transfer back to [Conway’s Game of Life](/interests/game-of-life/).
