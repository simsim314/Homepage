Conway’s Game of Life is a grid with a simple local rule and an extraordinary range of behavior. My work ranges from small seeds with quadratic growth to glider guns, signal conduits, oscillators, and self-constructing patterns. LifeAPI and CatForce provide the search infrastructure behind some of these discoveries.

Two major construction projects are [Remini](/articles/remini/), demonstrating modern single-channel self-construction with classic period-30 circuitry, and [Caterloopillar](/articles/caterloopilar/), whose two halves construct one another.

Also explore the 2014 quadratic-growth sequence, ending with switch engine ping-pong, and the B60 interaction behind the Simkin glider gun. The articles cover the discoveries listed on [my LifeWiki profile](https://conwaylife.com/wiki/Michael_Simkin), preserving shared credits and distinguishing historical records from current ones.
