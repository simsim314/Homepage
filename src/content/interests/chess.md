Chess is one of my hobbies. You can find me on [Lichess as simsim314](https://lichess.org/@/simsim314).

[Deep-Formix](/articles/deep-formix/) grew from stress-testing Gemini into a working GPU chess search and a custom deep position-evaluation network.

[Chess960×960](/articles/chess960x960/) is a browser tool for exploring independent Fischer Random starting positions for White and Black. Its Stockfish evaluator lets you inspect a position’s advantage or search for starting positions within an evaluation range. The article includes the interactive evaluator.
