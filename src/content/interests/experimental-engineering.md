Building is part of the research. This page brings together physical computation, computational models, and the software used to test an idea.

## Physical computation

[Still-life tiles](/articles/still-life-tiles/) turn Life’s stability constraints into pieces that fit together. The later [3D tile construction](/articles/life-in-3d-tiles/) encodes one evolving Life step through successive layers. [Multilayer Mutilated Gear](/articles/multilayer-mutilated-gear/) explores logic through a simulated mechanical system. Each article distinguishes models from physical experiments.

## Models and tools

[Simulation & Visualization](/interests/simulation-visualization/) follows deep fractals and mechanical models. [Programming & Computing](/interests/programming-computing/) follows the implementation work behind them, including GPU arithmetic, GPU chess, Life searches, and geometric algorithms.

## Practical experiments

The collection also includes [webcam-derived randomness](/articles/tqrng-webcam/) and a [desktop Wi-Fi QR-code reader](/articles/qr-wifi-scanner/). Their articles separate experimental claims from implemented conveniences.
