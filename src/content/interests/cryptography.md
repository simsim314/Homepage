Cryptography connects mathematical guarantees to devices and workflows. These projects explore two different uses of that connection.

## Signing data with a device-held key

[CryptoHanko](/articles/cryptohanko/) explores a digital stamp on Android: read QR data, sign it with a key held by the device, and let someone verify the resulting bundle. The article follows the implemented workflow and its experimental status.

## A cryptographic object you can hold

[CryptoCash](/articles/cryptocash/) investigates an NFC bearer-token proposal. Its circuit experiments explore how a constrained device might participate in a publicly verifiable system. The article distinguishes the proposal from the AES and witness-generation work already implemented.
