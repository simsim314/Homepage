Cryptography connects abstract protocols to devices you can hold. CryptoHanko implements a QR-based signing workflow on Android. CryptoCash investigates a different question: how a constrained NFC device might fit into a publicly verifiable system. The implemented app and the circuit experiment have different levels of maturity.
