I worked in computational geometry before moving into computer vision and AI. It is part of my wider interest in making ideas work through computation and engineering.

One project involved [computing a convex hull under tight memory constraints](/articles/read-only-convex-hull/), reading the input without keeping the whole input in working memory. The manuscript develops a randomized bridge-finding approach and iterative scans, with allocated space tied to the hull size.

The article explains the computational model, the bridge step, and the bounds stated in the manuscript.
