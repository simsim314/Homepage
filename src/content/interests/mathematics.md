This collection brings together my mathematical writing, including work in computational geometry. Each article identifies its status: a public preprint, an unpublished manuscript, or a research note.

Sphere packing drew my attention because the problem is easy to explain, even in high dimensions, while the mathematics behind it is complex. I first tried small-dimensional kissing problems, then moved toward mathematical results in large dimensions.

The [sphere-packing article](/articles/high-dimensional-sphere-packing/) follows that path through AI-assisted exploration and collaboration with Nihar Gargava and Matthias Gröbner. Our preprint gives a lattice-packing lower bound in every sufficiently large dimension.

My earlier work in [computational geometry](/interests/computational-geometry/) includes an unpublished manuscript on convex hulls with limited working memory.
