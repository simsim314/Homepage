My professional background is in AI research, with many years focused on computer vision. As AI has evolved, my interests have broadened, recently including AI-assisted mathematical proofs.

The published projects here explore what learned models can do inside specific computational systems. A neural cellular-automaton-like model evolves images of handwritten digits through local updates; Deep-Formix uses learned evaluation in a CUDA chess engine. Their articles describe the implementation and the limits of the available evidence.
